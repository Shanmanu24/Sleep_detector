What's EagleEye: EagleEye – The Sleep Detective is a driver-drowsiness detection web app with a sleek dark, neon-teal interface. It uses your webcam to monitor eye closure and triggers an alarm when your eyes stay shut too long. This browser-only preview relies on a simple brightness heuristic, so no installs or ML downloads are needed. Users calibrate a baseline, then watch live eye-zone brightness and alertness status ("Awake" or "Drowsy Alert"). The full version uses MediaPipe eye-landmark tracking with a Python/Flask backend for greater accuracy.
